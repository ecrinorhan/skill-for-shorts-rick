- **SFX** is an estimate: events found in a separated effects stem, with a heuristic class for the loudest one.

Camera notation: `hold` = the framing is steady apart from a slow drift, with the measured zoom rate in % per second (positive = pushing in); `MOVE` = a fast camera move, with its peak speed in % of frame height per second. A join is where two separately made takes meet: `cut`, or `dissolve` with its length in frames.
