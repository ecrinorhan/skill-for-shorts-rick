# Style bible: the numbers

Everything here was measured on the 20 newest uploads of the reference channel (all Shorts; the channel has no long videos). This file is generated by `analysis/scripts/25_style_bible.py` from `analysis/data/`, so the numbers match the data exactly.

**How to read the tables.** *Mean* and *Median* are across the 20 videos. *Middle 80 %* runs from the 10th to the 90th percentile: aim inside it. *Min – Max* is the full spread: staying inside it keeps you within what the channel itself does. Confidence: **measured** = read directly from the file; **estimate** = from separated audio stems, a heuristic classifier, or a property that YouTube's compression changes; **by eye** = one reviewer per video looked at frames (16 – 20 of the videos, stated per table).
