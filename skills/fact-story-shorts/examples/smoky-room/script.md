# Why Nobody Leaves the Smoky Room

255 words · 20 sentences · about 69 s at 222 words per minute · structure: claim hook, second-person scenario (Imagine), question, late explainer, last line is the sting of the explanation

**Fact core.** Latané and Darley (1968) put male Columbia University students in a waiting room to fill out a questionnaire and let smoke in through a wall vent. Alone, 75 % reported the smoke within the six-minute session; next to two actors who had been told to ignore it, 10 % did; in groups of three real participants, someone reported it in only 38 % of the groups. The authors' reading: people take other people's calm as a sign that nothing is wrong. DRAMATISED for this story: what 'you' think and do in the room, the stinging eyes, the clipboard, and how thick the smoke is at minute six.
