# The City That Paid for Rat Tails

**Fact core.** In 1902 the French colonial administration in Hanoi, afraid of plague, tried to clear the rats out of the city's new sewers. Hired rat catchers killed up to about 20,000 a day, which was not enough, so the city offered the public one cent per rat tail (tails only, to avoid handling the bodies). Officials then noticed live rats without tails; people were cutting tails off and letting the rats go to breed, bringing rats in from outside the city, and running rat farms on the outskirts. The bounty was cancelled, with the rat problem worse than before. Documented by the historian Michael G. Vann.
