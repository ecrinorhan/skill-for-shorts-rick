# Why Soldiers Break Step on Bridges

**Fact core.** A column marching in step can drive a suspension bridge at its natural frequency (resonance). On 12 April 1831 the Broughton Suspension Bridge near Manchester failed while 74 riflemen marched across it four abreast; a bolt in a stay chain snapped, about forty men fell into the river and twenty were hurt, none killed. The British Army then ordered troops to break step on bridges, and the order is still in use.
